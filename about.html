Company Profile

Welcome to Brightlands Diagnostics!

Incorporation date: 14/09/2020

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide an excellent high quality 128 Slice Routine CT scans / Cardiac CT, CT Coronary Angio, Ultrasound scans and other diagnostics tests experience for all our patients.

Our Vision

Our vision is to make health care more accessible, affordable and assuring for patients, our people are passionate, hardworking and committed to ensuring we deliver the highest patient care where possible. We are a leading specialist diagnostic services in Hyderabad. We can offer fast clinical access, expert diagnosis and world class reports from our fully qualified consultant radiologist.

Our Purpose & Priorities

Purpose: Driven by our passion for diagnostic services and providing world class Patient care experience to all.

Priorities: We will put great value and high-end technology at the heart of our business and building trust relations with all our patients.

Our Brand

At Brightlands we are proud of who we are, we're warm and human brand one that patient can trust to be straight forward and genuine at all times.

Our Values & Valued Behaviour

Core Values
  • Integrity: To be honest, trustworthy and consistent in our words
  • Respect: To recognize the intrinsic value and dignity of all individuals
  • Compassion: To respond to the feelings and needs of each person with kindness, concern and empathy
  • Excellence: To hold ourselves to the highest standards in all what we do
  • Leadership: To envision possibilities, seek opportunities, advocate and act to meet community and company needs
Valued Behaviour

Our Valued behaviour reflect how we all need to behave for us to deliver against our purpose and priorities, we call them 'valued behaviour' as they focused on the actions we all need to take and to highlight that we're each responsible for making a difference for our patients and colleagues.
